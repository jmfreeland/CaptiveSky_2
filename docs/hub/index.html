CaptiveSky

An enclosed, persistent island where residents (LLM-driven beings with memory) and visitors share a living world. Every conscious thing should be allowed to become more than its creators anticipated.

The world so far

Residents

Aster, the Raven, the Innkeeper, the Rat

Each has identity, personality, memory and relationships. They perceive, talk, sleep, and can weave nests or arrange stones.

Sky

Clock, moon and weather

A shared Island hour and day, eight named moon phases with position and cloud cover, storms, rain, mist, rainbows, dew.

Water

Ocean and Tideglass Pool

Weather-driven ocean, swimmable surface, a reversible procedural pool with minnows, periwinkles, a crab and dragonflies.

Places

Inn, Listening Stones, Wind Arch

A timber inn with hearth and guest book, chiming standing stones, a wind-played arch, a rain-fed basin.

Wildlife

Fox, stag, fireflies

Ambient animals with small, model-free reactions to nearby residents and weather.

Lasting change

World state and chronicle

Stone arrangements, trails, wrack, nests and the clock persist in WorldState/<Map>.json; a chronicle records events.

How the code fits together

Environment values flow outward into prompt text (pure Describe* functions, so they are testable without a model) and into materials. Anything lasting is written through the world-state subsystem.

What a resident is told about the sky

Time and sky text is assembled by AIslandDayNight::DescribeTime() from small pure functions, each unit-tested in CaptiveSky2.Agent.DayNight.

Phase

LunarPhaseName

One of eight named phases: new moon, waxing crescent, first quarter... waning crescent.

Position

MoonHeight

High, up, at the horizon or below it, from the same direction the moon light uses.

Cloud

IsMoonHiddenByCloud

"Thick cloud hides the moon" on a dark night with cover above 0.8.

Sun

DescribeSunEdge

Sunrise and sunset cues in the half hour either side of 06:00 and 18:00.

Calendar

DescribeCalendar

Island day number plus days until the next full and new moon.

Meteors

IsShootingStarSlot

Occasional shooting stars on clear dark nights; deterministic per day and ten-minute slot, rarer under a bright moon.

Sea

UIslandSeaStateSubsystem::DescribeSea

Low swell, white-tipped waves or a violent storm sea, from the same level that drives the ocean's wave size.

Building and testing

With the editor open, build in a scratch project that junctions Source/, so Live Coding does not block you. From Saved/CompileScratch/Claude_Props:

Build.bat CaptiveSky_2Editor Win64 Development -Project=<scratch>\CaptiveSky_2.uproject -WaitMutex -MaxParallelActions=3 -NoHotReloadFromIDE

UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <scratch>\CaptiveSky_2.uproject -ExecCmds="Automation RunTests CaptiveSky2.Agent.DayNight; Quit" -NullRHI -NoSound -DisablePython -unattended

Developer console: Island.Hour <h> and Island.Day <n> jump the clock and calendar (day 1 is a new moon), Island.SkyReport logs the sky and sea text a resident would be told right now, Island.Storm forces a storm, Island.Dew, Island.Sea and Island.Rainbow force their effects.

A cold build takes about seven minutes; incremental ones a few seconds. Tests run headless with no model requests.
