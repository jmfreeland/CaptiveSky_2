# Raven roost choice in wind (2026-10-01)

The raven asked for varied wind and quiet nesting places. His controller already exposes measured local wind and a read-only, geometry-based shelter clue, but opportunistic `BeginPerch()` always selected the nearest tagged roost, even during strong wind.

The prototype keeps the raven's choice of whether to wander or perch untouched. Once he chooses a perch, calm air (<85 cm/s at his current location) keeps the nearest physically supported roost. In stronger wind, each supported roost is scored by its measured local wind plus a small distance cost; a different site is selected only when it improves the score by more than 15 cm/s. Unsupported markers are excluded before selection and still must pass the existing physical landing check. This is only a local wind preference, not an assigned home, waterproof shelter, or guarantee of comfort. Missing weather preserves nearest-first selection.

The UE 5.8.3 scratch module compiled, and `CaptiveSky2.Agent.RavenPerch` passed. Its deterministic policy cases cover calm nearest-first selection, strong-wind preference for a meaningfully calmer nearby site, ignoring small exposure differences, and mismatched-sample safety. The test now also sweeps 1,200 simulated seconds of the saved Island's actual weather at 30-second intervals, sampling wind through each roost's real geometry. From the test raven's start, the current mild sample measured 54.0 cm/s, with West at 55.1 and East at 59.4; nearest-first chose East. Across the sweep, 16 samples exceeded the 85 cm/s strong-wind threshold: West was preferred in four, East in twelve. This confirms the rule can engage against the actual map layout, while still favoring the nearer site in most samples. The same run exercised saved-Island support checks and collision-swept west/east flights. This is deterministic weather-time sampling, not a rendered or lived gameplay observation. Next, use a bounded real-weather preview with background thinking disabled to judge the behavior visually before tuning; use a separate data root and keep the existing realtime/request caps.
