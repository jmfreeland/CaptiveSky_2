# Raven roost choice in wind (2026-10-01)

The raven asked for varied wind and quiet nesting places. His controller already exposes measured local wind and a read-only, geometry-based shelter clue, but opportunistic `BeginPerch()` always selected the nearest tagged roost, even during strong wind.

The prototype keeps the raven's choice of whether to wander or perch untouched. Once he chooses a perch, calm air (<85 cm/s at his current location) keeps the nearest physically supported roost. In stronger wind, each supported roost is scored by its measured local wind plus a small distance cost; a different site is selected only when it improves the score by more than 15 cm/s. Unsupported markers are excluded before selection and still must pass the existing physical landing check. This is only a local wind preference, not an assigned home, waterproof shelter, or guarantee of comfort. Missing weather preserves nearest-first selection.

The UE 5.8.3 scratch module compiled, and `CaptiveSky2.Agent.RavenPerch` passed. Its deterministic policy cases cover calm nearest-first selection, strong-wind preference for a meaningfully calmer nearby site, ignoring small exposure differences, and mismatched-sample safety. The same run also exercised the saved Island's support checks and collision-swept west/east flights. These results do not establish that the saved Island's west/east roosts differ enough in measured wind to trigger the preference during play. A bounded real-weather preview with background thinking disabled should inspect that before adjusting the thresholds; it should use a separate data root and keep the existing realtime/request caps.
