# Woodland stag responds to nearby thunder (2026-10-06)

The wild stag checks for active lightning strikes about every 0.35 seconds. It reacts only after sound has travelled from the strike point to the stag itself, and only when the strike is within 1.2 km. A close clap wakes a resting stag and briefly sends it away from the source, bounded by its existing 7 m home radius. Distant strikes are ignored, and one lightning actor cannot retrigger the response. The reaction is reversible movement only: no model request, ownership, or persistent world-state change.

Validation: UE 5.8.3 `CaptiveSky_2Editor` build succeeded. `CaptiveSky2.Agent.WoodlandDeer` passed with agent thinking disabled, `-CaptiveSkyMaxRealtimeSeconds=120`, and zero model requests. The regression verifies that the stag does not startle before its own sound delay even when the player would hear later, that a nearby clap causes one bounded startle, that a 1.5 km clap is ignored, and that nearby thunder wakes a resting stag.
