# Raven placeholder wing motion (2026-10-01)

The raven's locomotion state now drives the existing `LeftWing` and `RightWing` scene components. The controller caches their authored relative rotations when it possesses the pawn, then applies mirrored roll strokes from those immutable baselines: 46 degrees at 4.2 Hz for takeoff, 34 degrees at 3.8 Hz in flight, 20 degrees at 2.6 Hz while landing, and a smaller 16-degree, 2.4 Hz stroke while hopping. Grounded, perched, and resting states restore the authored rotations exactly. The two wing transforms are weakly held and a pawn without both named components is left unchanged. No Blueprint or Content asset was edited.

This verifies state-driven transform behavior, not the rendered appearance on the raven Blueprint.
