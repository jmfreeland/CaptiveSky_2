# Residents can notice nearby instanced plants

The asset-upgrade request action already accepts vegetation targets, but the ordinary nearby-prop summary intentionally excludes instanced components. Consequently, residents could suggest a world-wide vegetation change but had little factual context for a specific plant patch.

`UAgentBrainComponent::BuildSituationSummary` now checks visible HISM components owned by `AIslandWeather` within a 6.5 m radius. It uses each component's sphere-overlap query, examines at most 48 nearby instance transforms and traces visibility to at most three candidates per component, then reports no more than the three nearest distinct mesh names. The description uses the resident-relative direction and explicitly says this is descriptive `request_upgrade` context only—not a stable object ID or `move_to`/`interact` target. Hidden components and plants behind visibility-blocking geometry are omitted. It neither calls a model nor writes memory, world state, or the request queue.

The source-linked UE 5.8.3 scratch project built successfully with its own `Binaries` while the separate Claude editor remained open. `CaptiveSky2.Agent.NearbyVegetationPerception` passed under `NullRHI`, a 60-second real-time cap, disabled agent thinking, and a zero model-request cap. Its transient fixture verifies a nearby visible HISM mesh is described, its suggestion cannot become a movement or interaction target, a solid occluder suppresses it, and moving beyond the short radius removes it. Log: [`Codex_NearbyVegetationPerception_20261010.log`](../../Saved/Logs/Codex_NearbyVegetationPerception_20261010.log).

This test proves the bounded perception rules on a small fixture, not the cost or visibility of a full Island scatter with roughly 1.8 million instances, and not whether the incoming Fab plant names read naturally to residents. The next check is a matched prompt-construction timing sample on the saved Island after the editor is available; then validate the labels and visibility with one imported native plant asset. Until that evidence exists, the descriptive scan remains limited to the nearest 6.5 m.
