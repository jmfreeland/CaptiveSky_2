# Rat hollow: live Island audit

The user has confirmed the Rat's first home should be a **hidden fallen-wood hollow**, separate from the Raven's roost. This chooses the shelter concept, not a specific coordinate: the map still needs a concealed, traversable site survey before the Rat or a home marker is placed. The editor's live MCP endpoint is available on `127.0.0.1:8000`; `/Game/Maps/Island` is loaded.

## What exists now

- The Island has 187 actors (147 `StaticMeshActor`s). There is no Rat home marker or tagged hollow.
- `Roost_East_FallenWood` and `Roost_East_TrunkSupport` are part of the Raven roost, and both render `/Engine/BasicShapes/Cylinder.Cylinder`; they are stretched placeholder cylinders, not a hollow-log asset. Do not reuse them for the Rat.
- `Prop_InnFirewood` is `/Game/Generated/Tripo/FirewoodStack/FirewoodStack/StaticMeshes/FirewoodStack.FirewoodStack`; `Prop_TideglassBench` is the generated DriftwoodBench. Neither is a shelter.
- The PCG forest volume is at `(-80000, -80000, 500)` and spans roughly 250 m square. Live line traces found dense foliage there, but several apparently flat coarse-grid points were small high patches with water at log-scale offsets. No safe Rat anchor was chosen in that forest area.

## Provisional anchor to validate

The existing `NavMeshBoundsVolume_Agent` covers `x=-105900..-95900`, `y=95460..105460`. A quiet candidate within its bounds is `(-105000, 104750)`: a 5 m terrain grid has about 40 cm relief around a 10 m neighborhood; a 25-point 1 m grid measured 15.6 cm total relief over 4 m, with no water hits. The live editor view shows open brown ground with sparse low grass, not concealment. This candidate is roughly 50 m from the Inn and well away from the Raven roost; it needs a small foliage screen and an in-world camera/nav check before it becomes a resident location.

**Live recheck (2026-10-10):** an Unreal MCP viewport capture aimed at this point from `(-106000, 103250, 3200)` shows broad exposed brown ground, sparse low cover, and no nearby canopy. A center trace from Z=20,000 to -2,000 cm returned 17,669.5 cm, putting the hit at approximately Z=2,330.5 cm; this is consistent with the terrain survey, not a validation of shelter. The imported hollow was previewed at the candidate and its entrance/cavity and nest bed were visible from a close approach; the bark read pale in that preview. Six temporary Common Hazel skeletal-mesh variants showed bare branch structures without foliage, so they did not provide concealment. The `ProceduralVegetationEditor` and `PCG` plugins are enabled in the connected editor. A project-local copy of the Common Hazel PVE graph exists at `/Game/Generated/ProceduralVegetation/PVE_Common_Hazel_01`; its generator preview is leafy, but the previous preview reported about 389k triangles and 923 foliage instances, while the exported tree skeletal meshes are branch-only. I built `/Game/Generated/ProceduralVegetation/PCG_RatHollowUnderstory_Prototype` from four separate 256-triangle leaf-cluster meshes: 32 surveyed PCG points with HISM output, collision/navigation/shadow disabled, and no execution warnings after setting the points' invalid metadata entry to `-1`. The graph yields about 8.2k source triangles at most. A temporary viewport composition showed the rear arc obscures most of the hollow while keeping the -X entrance gap clear, but the enlarged leaves look oversized and too vivid against the bare soil. The temporary PCG volume and hollow actors were removed and no map changes were saved. Unreal still reports the Island package dirty after the add/remove cycle; loading it again is refused until the unsaved state is resolved. Do not assign this point as the Rat's home until the foliage scale/site blending, concealed approach, and navigation are validated.

**Scale retest (2026-10-10):** I halved all 32 point scales in the saved PCG prototype (5.8–6.85 became 2.9–3.43) and reran it with the imported hollow in a temporary Island composition. Execution returned no warnings. At close range the ring still reads as regularly spaced oversized leaf plates rather than natural understory, and the exposed mud around it leaves the approach visible. Both temporary actors were removed; the current map was not saved. This is a useful low-cost prototype, not a Rat home placement.

**PN foliage-pack iteration (2026-10-10):** I replaced the leaf plates with four existing local meshes (`ground_05_01`, `ground_06_02`, `ground_11_01`, `ground_12_01`), weighted 3:3:2:2. Their LOD0 counts are 208, 267, 230, and 362 triangles. The saved graph now emits 64 jittered instances in the same C-shaped understory layout, with per-point scale 1.55–3.15; the entrance gap remains open. HISM descriptors retain `NoCollision`, navigation export disabled, and shadows disabled. The weighted mix is at most 23,168 source triangles, before LOD effects. Graph execution returned no warnings. The close viewport read is more plant-like than the leaf-plate version, but it still forms an isolated ring on exposed mud rather than a concealed, blended habitat. The preview PCG volume and hollow actors were removed; the Island was not saved. The local graph asset is saved under `/Game/Generated/ProceduralVegetation/` and is gitignored with project Content.

## Asset direction

`Scripts/AssetPipeline/assets/rathollow.py` defines an original 2.6 m weathered trunk, a 0.78 m entrance, a sheltered straw bed, restrained moss patches, and collision strips that leave the passage open. It uses the local ComfyBlender pipeline and shared textures; it does not call Tripo or consume API credits. The 2026-10-10 manifest bounds are 256 x 136 x 134 cm, about 1,836 triangles, with four `UBX_` collision strips. The live editor imported it to `/Game/Generated/ComfyBlender/RatHollow/SM_RatHollow_01`; `unreal_import.json` reports 256.4 x 135.7 x 134.1 cm, all four material slots assigned, four box collision shapes, and no validation errors. The source explicitly leaves the hollow tunnel open to its dark backing, and the close-up editor preview showed the cavity and straw bed; the earlier cap-blocked audit is superseded. This validates the mesh, not its final placement: the open candidate remains exposed and the mesh appeared pale in the viewport. Do not assign coordinates to the Rat's lived-memory `places.json` until a concealed site and traversable approach pass live checks.

The editor was running the `captivesky_2_claude` worktree, whose `Saved/` directory does not contain this generated source pack, while its `Content/` is junctioned to the main checkout. The importer now accepts an explicit `COMFYBLENDER_SOURCE_ROOT` for this legitimate multi-checkout case; import output still goes to the currently open project's `/Game/Generated/ComfyBlender/`.
