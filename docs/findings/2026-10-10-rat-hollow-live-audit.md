# Rat hollow: live Island audit

The user has confirmed the Rat's first home should be a **hidden fallen-wood hollow**, separate from the Raven's roost. This chooses the shelter concept, not a specific coordinate: the map still needs a concealed, traversable site survey before the Rat or a home marker is placed. The editor's live MCP endpoint is available on `127.0.0.1:8000`; `/Game/Maps/Island` is loaded.

## What exists now

- `Roost_East_FallenWood` and `Roost_East_TrunkSupport` are part of the Raven roost, and both render `/Engine/BasicShapes/Cylinder.Cylinder`; they are stretched placeholder cylinders, not a hollow-log asset. Do not reuse them for the Rat.

- The PCG forest volume is at `(-80000, -80000, 500)` and spans roughly 250 m square. Live line traces found dense foliage there, but several apparently flat coarse-grid points were small high patches with water at log-scale offsets. No safe Rat anchor was chosen in that forest area.

## Asset direction

`Scripts/AssetPipeline/assets/rathollow.py` defines an original 2.6 m weathered trunk, a 0.78 m entrance, a sheltered straw bed, restrained moss patches, and collision strips that leave the passage open. It uses the local ComfyBlender pipeline and shared textures; it does not call Tripo or consume API credits. The 2026-10-10 manifest bounds are 256 x 136 x 134 cm, about 1,836 triangles, with four `UBX_` collision strips. The live editor imported it to `/Game/Generated/ComfyBlender/RatHollow/SM_RatHollow_01`; `unreal_import.json` reports 256.4 x 135.7 x 134.1 cm, all four material slots assigned, four box collision shapes, and no validation errors. The source explicitly leaves the hollow tunnel open to its dark backing, and the close-up editor preview showed the cavity and straw bed; the earlier cap-blocked audit is superseded. This validates the mesh, not its final placement: the open candidate remains exposed and the mesh appeared pale in the viewport. Do not assign coordinates to the Rat's lived-memory `places.json` until a concealed site and traversable approach pass live checks.

The editor was running the `captivesky_2_claude` worktree, whose `Saved/` directory does not contain this generated source pack, while its `Content/` is junctioned to the main checkout. The importer now accepts an explicit `COMFYBLENDER_SOURCE_ROOT` for this legitimate multi-checkout case; import output still goes to the currently open project's `/Game/Generated/ComfyBlender/`.
