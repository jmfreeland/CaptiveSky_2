# Tideglass Minnows

The shallow Tideglass pool now has one small, visual-only minnow school during the Island day (06:00–19:00). `AIslandWeather` owns its lifetime alongside the other habitat routines; repeated refreshes reuse the same school, dusk removes it, and dawn creates one again. The five fish have no collision and are not movement targets. Small tail fins wag asynchronously, and stable blue-grey, sea-glass, and muted green-silver color variants make individuals easier to distinguish without implying separate species or increasing the population.

The fish trace a compact loop and tighten their spread a little during heavy rain. A nearby visitor or resident may quietly watch them: the school fans away for a couple of seconds, then returns to its local path. This response changes no saved state, transfers no ownership, and makes no model requests. Residents only receive it as optional nearby wildlife context, and may ignore it entirely.

This is a habitat prototype, not a simulation of tide, feeding, reproduction, or water quality. It uses the pool marker as its center and does not infer depth or safe habitat from the marker. The fish remain lightweight primitive-mesh stand-ins; their tail silhouette and individual coloring are a first visual pass rather than imported final art. `CaptiveSky2.Agent.TidepoolMinnows` covers the bounded population, local movement, tail animation and appearance, scatter/regroup response, visible visitor interaction, resident inspection, rain response, and day/night lifecycle without a live play session.
