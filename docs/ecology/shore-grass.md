# Shore grass

At world start, the weather actor scatters deterministic clumps of two existing grass meshes in rings around Tideglass Pool, the Listening Stones, Wind Arch, and the inn entrance. The clumps follow walkable ground, do not collide or affect navigation, and sway from the local wind field. A Wind Arch gust therefore changes the grass nearby without changing island-wide weather. Tideglass and Listening Stones each use 96 candidates on an annulus 2.6–7.2 m from their marker; the Wind Arch verge uses 192 candidates and the inn uses 72 candidates on wider 3.5–12 m approach rings. The inn ring also rejects steep or ungrounded candidates, ground far above/below the door marker, and any candidate without open sky, preventing grass on roofs or inside the building. The current absolute transient-instance ceiling is 456, before terrain, pool-edge, and roof filters discard candidates.

The Oct 1 density pass doubles the original 48 candidates per landmark. The paired 8:00 renders showed the lower count reading as isolated tufts, with the wind response difficult to see. The higher count aims for a more connected, readable verge while keeping the foliage as lightweight HISM instances and leaving open the landmark centers. Recheck the resulting in-engine image before further density increases; screenshots alone do not establish runtime performance on the target machine.

The Oct 1 wind pass raises the response ceiling from six to ten degrees and gives local gusts a more legible bend without changing the wind field or persistent weather. The fixed Tideglass preview gusts measured 9.37 degrees at peak. The automated capture assertion and ground-cover unit test both enforce the ten-degree bound; the transforms continue to derive from immutable planted baselines, so sway does not accumulate or translate the grass bases.

`CaptiveSky2.Agent.GroundCover` covers deterministic placement, ground alignment, nonblocking/navigation flags, bounded wind sway, local gust response, stable immutable baselines, idempotent initialization, cleanup, and an inn-roof/open-sky fixture. A rendered preview can be captured with `-ViewpointGroundCover`; add `-ViewpointGroundCoverSway` to preview a local gust around the landmark and inn rings.

## Pool-edge clearance (2026-10-01)

The October 1 Game spectator frames showed grass silhouettes crossing the pool in screen space. This may have been foreground occlusion rather than plants rooted in the water, so the runtime scatter now derives a conservative exclusion radius from the flattened source sphere's world bounds, adds the procedural outline and a 125 cm vegetation/marker margin, then filters Tideglass candidates while leaving ListeningStones placement unchanged. The focused UE 5.8.3 `CaptiveSky2.Agent.GroundCover` test passed with the matching shallow-sphere fixture: it computed a 464 cm fixture clearance, placed 152 clumps (76 per species), preserved both verges, and found no grass inside the exclusion area. The real Tideglass actor reports a 306 cm clearance. The real-RHI `CaptiveSky2.Visual.Viewpoints` test also passed and captured Tideglass with ground cover enabled; the pool edge is open and the verge remains visible beyond it. This is a visual placement check, not a packaged performance test.
