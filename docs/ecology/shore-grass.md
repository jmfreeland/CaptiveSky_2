# Shore grass

At world start, the weather actor scatters deterministic clumps of two existing grass meshes in rings around Tideglass Pool and the Listening Stones. The clumps follow walkable ground, do not collide or affect navigation, and sway from the local wind field. A Wind Arch gust therefore changes the grass nearby without changing island-wide weather. The current scatter is 96 candidate clumps per landmark (at most 192 total) on an annulus 2.6–7.2 m from each landmark; steep or ungrounded candidates are discarded.

The Oct 1 density pass doubles the original 48 candidates per landmark. The paired 8:00 renders showed the lower count reading as isolated tufts, with the wind response difficult to see. The higher count aims for a more connected, readable verge while keeping the foliage as lightweight HISM instances and leaving open the landmark centers. Recheck the resulting in-engine image before further density increases; screenshots alone do not establish runtime performance on the target machine.

`CaptiveSky2.Agent.GroundCover` covers deterministic placement, ground alignment, nonblocking/navigation flags, bounded wind sway, local gust response, stable immutable baselines, idempotent initialization, and cleanup. A rendered preview can be captured with `-ViewpointGroundCover`; add `-ViewpointGroundCoverSway` to preview a local gust around the two landmark rings.

## Density and wind validation (2026-10-01)

After the density increase, the UE 5.8.3 `CaptiveSky2.Agent.GroundCover` and `CaptiveSky2.Agent.NightEcology` automation tests passed.
