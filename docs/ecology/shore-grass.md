# Shore grass

At world start, the weather actor scatters deterministic clumps of two existing grass meshes plus one broadleaf ground-plant mesh in local rings around Tideglass Pool, the Listening Stones, Wind Arch, and the inn entrance, then adds wider patches over exposed Island landscape. Instances follow walkable ground, do not collide or affect navigation, and sway from the local wind field. A Wind Arch gust therefore changes nearby plants without changing island-wide weather. Tideglass and Listening Stones each use 144 local candidates on an annulus 2.6–7.2 m from their marker; Wind Arch uses 288 candidates and the inn 108 candidates on wider 3.5–12 m approach rings. One quarter of candidates use the broadleaf plant, normalized to about 70 cm tall from its actual mesh bounds. The inn ring also rejects steep or ungrounded candidates, ground far above/below the door marker, and any candidate without open sky, preventing plants on roofs or inside the building. The local-ring ceiling is 684 instances; the landscape layer is bounded at 128 patches × 128 candidates (16,384), for a combined 17,068 transient instances before terrain, pool-edge, slope, and roof filters discard candidates.

The Oct 1 density pass doubles the original 48 candidates per landmark. The paired 8:00 renders showed the lower count reading as isolated tufts, with the wind response difficult to see. The higher count aims for a more connected, readable verge while keeping the foliage as lightweight HISM instances and leaving open the landmark centers. Recheck the resulting in-engine image before further density increases; screenshots alone do not establish runtime performance on the target machine.

The Oct 1 wind pass raises the response ceiling from six to ten degrees and gives local gusts a more legible bend without changing the wind field or persistent weather. The fixed Tideglass preview gusts measured 9.37 degrees at peak. The automated capture assertion and ground-cover unit test both enforce the ten-degree bound; the transforms continue to derive from immutable planted baselines, so sway does not accumulate or translate the grass bases.

`CaptiveSky2.Agent.GroundCover` covers deterministic placement, ground alignment, nonblocking/navigation flags, bounded wind sway, local gust response, stable immutable baselines, idempotent initialization, cleanup, and an inn-roof/open-sky fixture. A rendered preview can be captured with `-ViewpointGroundCover`; add `-ViewpointGroundCoverSway` to preview a local gust around the landmark and inn rings.
